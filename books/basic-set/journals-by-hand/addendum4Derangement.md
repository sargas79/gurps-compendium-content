Derangement represents lasting mental harm. At the end of a day that inflicts no new Stress or Derangement, someone with Derangement may roll against Will. Success means they shed -1 Derangement. The care of a clinician with Psychology (Clinical) or Physician (Psychiatric) at 12+ gives +1 to this roll – and the therapist can roll against their skill once a day to erase another -1 Derangement.

The GM may apply Derangement/2, rounded for the worst, as a penalty to Influence rolls as well as to Fright Checks. It’s one thing to appear stable when sitting around (explaining why there’s no reaction penalty), but a loose grip on reality is harder to hide when actively trying to manipulate others, who find the influencer pitiful, disturbing, or evil, depending on their beliefs. This penalty also affects any roll required to establish sanity; e.g., before a judge. It might even affect rolls for supernatural powers, penalizing the use of those that require a focused mind or chi, and resistance against (but a bonus of equal size to use!) evil ones that cause further Derangement. Fearlessness never offsets Derangement.

**Derangement Limit:** A character’s Derangement limit is -Will. Convert Derangement gains beyond that into points in permanent mental disadvantages!
