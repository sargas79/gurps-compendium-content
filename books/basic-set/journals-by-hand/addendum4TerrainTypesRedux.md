Foraging, tracking, and overland travel – and Survival specialties – depend on terrain in complex ways that various supplements render more complex. In cinematic games, and realistic ones in which these activities matter but not enough to be picky, use this quick-and-dirty summary.

**Terrain:** The terrain type, named after the Survival specialty (p. 224) required there.

**Foraging:** Modifier to the rolls in Foraging (p. 427). 1d-7 gives from -6 to -1; 2d-7 gives from -5 to +5. To save time, use the listed average.

**Tracking:** Modifier to Tracking (p. 226) rolls.

**Travel Speed:** Multipliers used for daily travel (p. 351) in typical conditions. To save time, use the listed average.

| Terrain | Foraging | Tracking | Travel Speed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arctic | 1d-7 (avg. -4) | -2\* | ×0.20 (loose snow), ×0.50 (slippery ice), or ×1 (hard-packed snow); avg. ×0.50† |
| Desert | 1d-7 (avg. -4) | -2\* | ×0.20 (loose sand) to ×1.25 (hard-packed sand); avg. ×0.50 |
| Island/Beach | 2d-7 (avg. 0) | -2\* | ×0.20 (loose sand) to ×1.25 (hard rock); avg. ×0.50 |
| Jungle | 2d-7 (avg. 0) | 0 | ×0.20 |
| Mountain | 1d-7 (avg. -4) | -2 | ×0.20 |
| Plains | 2d-7 (avg. 0) | 0 | ×0.50 (hilly) to ×1.25 (completely level); avg. ×1.00 |
| Swampland | 2d-7 (avg. 0) | -4 | ×0.20 |
| Woodlands | 2d-7 (avg. 0) | 0 | ×0.20 (dense forest) to ×1.00 (light forest); avg. ×0.50 |

\* In loose snow without wind (Arctic), loose sand without wind (Desert), or loose sand without lapping water (Island/Beach), use 0; with lots of wind or water, use -4.

† Skiers get ×1 on snow; skaters get ×1.25 on ice.
