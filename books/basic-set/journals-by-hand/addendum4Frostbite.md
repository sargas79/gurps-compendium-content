Frostbite is the freezing of exposed flesh. As a simplification, assume that the FP and HP losses to Cold (p. 430) cover this . . . but in reality, the acute somatic damage of frostbite and the metabolic damage of hypothermia are distinct, and can and do occur simultaneously! In a harshly realistic campaign, the GM may rule that adventurers in freezing conditions lose 1 HP on any exposed hit location per FP lost to cold. This can cripple extremities or even limbs, and is cumulative with injury due to hypothermia. To prevent this, wear gloves on the hands and a balaclava, mask, scarf, or deep hood to protect the face.
