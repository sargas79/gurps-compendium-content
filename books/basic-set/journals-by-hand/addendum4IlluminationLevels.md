While pp. 358, 548 assess from -1 to -9 to Vision and ranged attack rolls for partial darkness, these penalties aren’t associated with concrete illumination levels. This table ties them to common examples of natural and artificial light.

| Penalty\* | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| -10 | Total darkness |
| -9 | Overcast moonless night |
| -8 | Starlight through clouds (moonless night) |
| -7 | Starlight (moonless night) |
| -6 | Quarter moon† |
| -5 | Half moon†, indicator LED‡ |
| -4 | Full moon† |
| -3 | Deep twilight†, candlelight‡, nightlight‡ |
| -2 | Twilight†, street lights (side road), gaslight‡, cell-phone screen‡ |
| -1 | Sunrise or sunset, street lights (main road), torch‡, standard flashlight‡ |
| 0§ | Daylight from very overcast day to full sun, ordinary interior light or brighter (TV studio, surgical theater, etc.), reading light‡, high-quality flashlight‡ |

\* Modifiers assume steady illumination. Flickering lights or moving shadows add an extra -1 to -3. Human eyes take time to adapt to sudden darkness (2 minutes per sudden shift of -1); apply another -2 until adapted.

† Under a clear sky. Apply another -1 for heavy cloud. Above the Arctic Circle or below the Antarctic, moonlight is weaker; add an extra -1.

‡ Light source is a single, nearby point. Listed modifier assumes 1 yard of distance. At 2 yards, apply another -1. At greater distances, add half the range penalty (p. 550), rounded for the worse, to this -1. For instance, a candle gives -3 at 1 yard, -4 at 2 yards, -5 out to 5 yards, -6 out to 10 yards, and so on. Stop once the penalty reaches the area’s ambient illumination level – a light source never makes things darker!

§ Bright light never grants Vision bonuses – but a light source itself is always seen at +10 (“in plain sight,” below), and some tasks, like surgery, may need exceptionally bright light to avoid an extra -1 or -2.

The total of all penalties mentioned above – for darkness, flickering, shadow, cloud, a distant source, incomplete adaptation, etc. – cannot go past -10. Treat anything worse than -10 as -10.
