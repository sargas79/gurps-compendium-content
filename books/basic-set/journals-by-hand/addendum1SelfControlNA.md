Self-Control for Mental Disadvantages (p. 120) allows controlling one’s urges on 15, 12, 9, or 6 or less on 3d. However, for the Disadvantage enhancement on Affliction (p. 36), the Temporary Disadvantage limitation on advantages (p. 115), and beings that always behave in some way (demons do evil, zombies rampage, robots follow programming, etc.), self-control rolls or spending points to succeed at them might not make sense.

In these cases, select a mental disadvantage that requires a self-control roll, specify a self-control number of “N/A,” and price the disadvantage at 2.5 times the listed cost (drop fractions). Berserk is unsuitable except as an Affliction or Temporary Disadvantage. The GM’s word is final in other cases.

Also at the GM’s option, mundane characters may have this level to represent neurological rather than psychological qualities. If so, these are physical (P) – not mental (M) – for all purposes.
