The social benefits of Clerical Investment (p. 43), Legal Enforcement Powers (p. 65), Legal Immunity (p. 65), Security Clearance (p. 82), Tenure (p. 93), and all similar advantages “just work” when the possessor is in their jurisdiction, proves their legitimacy (via badge, papers, uniform, etc.), and follows procedure – don’t make Assistance Rolls for the reaction bonuses, powers, and perquisites of such traits! Claim to Hospitality (p. 41) requires a roll, but that isn’t an AR. And pay attendant to Rank requires a job roll (p. 516), not an AR.
