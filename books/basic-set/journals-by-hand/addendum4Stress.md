Stress represents temporary jitters. It bleeds off at -1 per 10 minutes spent in mental relaxation, defined as any rest that allows FP recovery (p. 427), or pursuing any leisure activity that gets +4 or better for “routine tasks” (p. 345), from target shooting to a Sunday drive. Much as a meal while resting restores +1 FP, the GM may let indulgences (dance, drink, massage, etc.) erase an extra -1 Stress.

The GM is welcome to apply Stress/2, rounded for the worst, as a penalty not only to Fright Checks, but also to self-control rolls (Bad Temper flares, someone with Gluttony overindulges, and so on), to skill rolls that demand a steady hand (e.g., Explosives and Surgery), and even to HT rolls to resist disease (stress suppresses the immune system). Reduce these penalties by Fearlessness level, but this never gives a net bonus except to Fright Checks.

**Stress Limit:** A character’s Stress limit is -Will (e.g., -11 for Will 11). Convert Stress gains beyond that into Derangement.
