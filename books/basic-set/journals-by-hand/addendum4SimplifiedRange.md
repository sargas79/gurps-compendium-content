Lookups on the Size and Speed/Range Table (p. 550) slow combat. Particularly when not using a map, the GM may prefer to divide range into a series of broad bands:

| Range Band | Penalty | Actual Ranges | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Close | 0\* | 0-5 yards | Melee or close-quarters engagement. |
| Short | -3 | 6-20 yards | Pistol engagement. |
| Medium | -7 | 21-100 yards | Shotgun or SMG engagement. |
| Long | -11 | 101-500 yards | Rifle engagement. |
| Extreme | -15 | 501+ yards | Sniper engagement. |

\* Bulk penalties apply if combatants are close enough to punch and grapple.

Use mean actual range in yards, if known, to determine a fight’s starting range band. If you don’t know this, use the description or common sense to assign a band; e.g., when kicking down a door, it’s safe to assume Close!

Fighters at Close and Short can move between these bands by taking a Move or Move and Attack maneuver on their turn. Action at Medium range or above stays there.
