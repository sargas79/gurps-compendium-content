Tracking ammunition, batteries (see box), and so forth can be tedious. The GM may opt to tally the cost and weight of five full reloads for firearms – or one full quiver or pouch of low-tech ammo – for each PC’s weapons. Include sufficient magazines, quivers, speedloaders, or similar containers to hold it. At the beginning of the adventure, subtract the cost from budgets and add the weight to encumbrance. Then ignore detailed ammo tracking until one of the following happens:

- The party is cut off. Then everybody has five reloads left and must track ammo.

- Someone objects to the encumbrance and ditches some ammo. From that point on, they have only what they didn’t drop.

- A PC is captured. They then have no ammo or weapons!

This never applies to explosives (rockets, grenades, etc.), fine or magical ammo, or anything but generic arrows, bullets, and so on. Always track such exceptional resources!

For batteries and cells, assume that all gizmos have power enough for a day. After that, every $3, 1 lb. at TL6-8 – or $10, 0.5 lb. at TL9+ – of spares carried gives one person an extra day of power. After that, nothing that requires power works unless the GM feels generous.
