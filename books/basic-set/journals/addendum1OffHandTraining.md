You can ignore the -4 for using the “off” hand (see Handedness, p. 14) with a specific skill, specified when you buy the perk. Any skill with applications that normally use one hand – including combat skills – qualifies. Benefits extend to all defenses and techniques based on the skill.

This perk completely replaces the Off-Hand Weapon Training technique (p. 232) – paying 5 points to remove -4 for one skill that way is a bad deal next to Ambidexterity! In this vein, if you eventually acquire five Off-Hand Training perks, the GM may let you exchange them for Ambidexterity [5].
