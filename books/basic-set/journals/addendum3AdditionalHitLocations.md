Combat-heavy campaigns benefit from more hit locations. Below, Major Wounds (p. 420) cause the usual HT rolls to avoid Knockdown and Stunning (p. 420). Crippling injuries count as major wounds and render the body part useless. For recovery, see Duration of Crippling Injuries (p. 422) – but dismemberment (p. 421) is permanent!

**Ear (-7):** Valid target whenever the face or skull is. Treat as face except for a cutting attack specifically to slice off the ear. Then injury over HP/4 is lost with no special effect – but twice this amount removes the ear, a major wound without the face’s -5 to knockdown rolls. Missing ears (one or both) permanently reduce Appearance by a level. Misses by 1 hit the torso (chest).

**Nose (-7):** Valid target whenever the front of the face is. Treat as face except that injury over HP/4 breaks the nose, a major wound to the face that gives the victim No Sense of Smell/Taste (p. 146) until the injury heals. For a cutting attack specifically to lop off the nose, crippling injury counts as a major wound without the face’s -5 to knockdown rolls, and injury in excess of this is lost – but twice this amount removes the nose, reducing Appearance by two levels permanently. Misses by 1 hit the torso (chest).

**Jaw (-6):** Valid target whenever the front of the face is. Treat as face, except that crushing injury gives an extra -1 to knockdown rolls. Misses by 1 hit the torso (chest).

**Chest (0) and Abdomen (-1):** Optionally, separate the torso into chest (areas 9-10), the breast and upper back, and abdomen (replacing the groin as area 11 when rolling randomly), including the stomach and groin. Treat as torso, but torso armor often protects only the chest – see its description.

**Spine (-8):** Valid target for crushing, cutting, impaling, piercing, and tight-beam burning attacks whenever the back of the neck or torso (including chest or abdomen) is. Vertebrae provide an additional DR 3. Use the torso’s wounding modifiers – but any hit that inflicts shock requires a knockdown roll, at -5 if a major wound. Injury in excess of HP cripples the spine, causing automatic knockdown and stunning, plus the effects of Bad Back (Severe) (p. 123) and Lame (Paraplegic) (p. 141). Roll twice to recover, once to avoid gaining each of these disadvantages on a lasting or permanent basis! Misses by 1 hit the torso.

**Pelvis (-3):** Valid target whenever the abdomen or leg is. Treat as torso, but on a major wound, the victim falls down, cannot stand, and can fight only if they assume a sitting or lying posture. Until healed, treat as Lame (Missing Legs) (p. 141). Protected by any armor specified as covering the abdomen or the torso and groin. Misses by 1 hit the torso (abdomen).

**Joints (-5 or -7):** Valid target for any crushing, cutting, piercing, or tight-beam burning attack to a limb or extremity. Gives an extra -3 to hit: -5 for arm or leg, -7 for hand or foot. Allows crippling with injury over HP/3 (not HP/2) for a limb or HP/4 (not HP/3) for an extremity; excess injury is lost. Rolls to recover from crippling are at -2. Dismemberment still requires twice the injury needed to cripple the whole body part – not just the joint. Misses by 1 hit the limb or extremity, not the joint.

**Veins/Arteries (-5 or -8):** Valid target for any cutting, impaling, piercing, or tight-beam burning attack to the neck, arm, or leg. Gives an extra -3 to hit: -5 for a limb, -8 for the neck. Increase the hit location’s wounding modifier by 0.5 – e.g., cutting damage gets ×2 instead of ×1.5 against a limb, or ×2.5 instead of ×2 for the neck – but ignore crippling effects and injury limits for limbs. The GM may rule that Mortal Wounds (p. 423) doesn’t apply – any failed HT roll to avoid death means collapsing and bleeding to death! Misses by 1 hit the neck, arm, or leg, as appropriate.
