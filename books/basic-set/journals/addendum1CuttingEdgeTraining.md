You’ve received instruction beyond your personal TL in one specific technological skill; e.g., Cutting-Edge Training (Piloting/TL8 (Aerospace)) for a TL7 test pilot. If the setting spans many TLs, the GM may allow multiple levels; e.g., Cutting-Edge Training 2 (Piloting/TL9 (Aerospace)) [2].

Cutting-Edge Training is cheaper than High TL (p. 23) for people with fewer than five specialties. Should you eventually acquire five such perks, the GM may let you exchange them for High TL 1 [5].

This perk often has social prerequisites like Duty, Rank, or Security Clearance.
