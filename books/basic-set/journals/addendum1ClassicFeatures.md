You embody certain features, specified when you buy this perk; e.g., you’re markedly pale or tan, or the epitome of blondes or redheads, or have idealized ethnic or racial looks. When you interact with an NPC who fancies those looks for some reason (often a quirk), treat your Appearance as one level higher, cumulative with any specified reaction bonus. For instance, someone with the quirk “Prefers redheads (+2 reactions)” treats a Classic Redhead with Average looks as Attractive (+1) and reacts at +2 for their quirk.
