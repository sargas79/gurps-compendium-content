**Hard**

Defaults: prerequisite skill-4, -8, or -12.\* Prerequisite: Any Melee Weapon skill; cannot remove more than half the default penalty.\* The GM may let fighters use weapons without “C” in their Reach statistic in close combat (see Weapons for Close Combat, p. 391) at penalties based on their longest reach – not the reach at which they’re currently ready. Skill is at -4 for reach 1, -8 for reach 2, or -12 for reach 3, and Parry is based on reduced skill. Swing damage is at -1 per yard of reach; e.g., -1 for a broadsword, -2 for a greatsword, or -3 for a halberd. Thrusting attacks do normal damage. (Ignore these rules for hilt and pommel strikes, Armed Grapple, and other tactics that assume close combat.)

This technique lets you buy off half the skill penalty. You may improve it to skill-2 for a reach 1 weapon, skill-4 for a reach 2 weapon, or skill-6 for a reach 3 weapon.

\* Close Combat is also available for ranged weapons, letting you buy off the entire Bulk penalty for close-combat purposes only.
