These options follow the same general rules as those on p. 357.

Giant Step: If you take an Attack or Defensive Attack maneuver (but not an All-Out Attack, Committed Attack, or Move and Attack, which already allow extra movement), you may spend 1 FP for one extra step. This can come before or after your attack – you can step, attack, and step again. Critical failure on the attack roll causes 1 HP of injury to the leg, not the arm.

Great Lunge: With an Attack, Committed Attack, or Move and Attack maneuver (but not an All-Out Attack or Defensive Attack) in melee combat, you may spend 1 FP to get +1 to your attack’s reach.

Heroic Charge: If you make a Move and Attack, you may spend 1 FP to ignore both its skill penalty and – in melee combat – its effective skill cap. Defensive drawbacks still apply: no parry, no retreat, -2 to rolls to avoid falling or tripping, etc.

Rapid Recovery: If you attack with an unbalanced weapon (“U” on Parry) during an Attack maneuver – or with any weapon using Move and Attack – and a foe later attacks you, you may spend 1 FP to parry with that weapon. You must spend FP before you try your first parry, but unless this parry critically fails, the weapon can continue to parry afterward, at the usual penalties.

You may use no more than one “offensive” option (Flurry of Blows, Giant Step, Great Lunge, Heroic Charge, or Mighty Blows) and one “defensive” option (Feverish Defense or Rapid Recovery) per turn.
