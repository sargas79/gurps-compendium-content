At the GM’s option, extra effort can aid focus and concentration, granting a one-time bonus to a success roll. This costs a flat 1 FP per +1 (maximum +4), requires no Will roll to invoke, and so poses no risk of catastrophic failure. The GM decides whether attacks may use this rule or are limited to All-Out Attack (Determined) – and whether tasks that require concentration may use this rule or must use All-Out Concentrate (p. 575).
