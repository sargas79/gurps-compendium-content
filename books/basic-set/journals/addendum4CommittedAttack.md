This middle ground between Attack and All-Out Attack is an aggressive attack, such as a lunge or roundhouse. You must use a ready melee attack and choose one of these options before attacking:

- Determined: Make a single attack at +2 to hit.

- Strong: Make a single attack at normal skill and +1 damage – or +1 damage per two full dice of basic damage, if that would be better. This applies only to ST-based damage – not to force swords, etc.

Movement: Step or two steps. A second step gives -2 to hit, making the total modifier +0 for Committed Attack (Determined) or -2 for Committed Attack (Strong), and you can move before or after attacking – or step, attack, and step again.

Active Defense: You cannot parry with the hand(s) you use to attack, block if you attack with your shield or cloak, or dodge if you kick. You can use any other defense, but at -2. You cannot retreat.
