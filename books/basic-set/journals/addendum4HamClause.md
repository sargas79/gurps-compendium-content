A player can willingly have their PC suffer from one of their disadvantages during a pivotal scene. Ignore the standard game mechanics. Instead, all success rolls for the hero during that encounter are at -1 per -5 points (or fraction thereof) of disadvantage value; e.g., in front of a crowd, someone with Lecherousness (12) [-15] might show off for attractive bystanders, suffering -3 for an entire battle, chase, standoff, etc. The GM must agree that the proposal makes sense – but if they do, their side of the bargain is to neither inflict the disadvantage again that session nor penalize the player for bad roleplaying.
