Prerequisite: Attribute or skill at 16+.

You’re exempt from skill or attribute rolls to perform a specific background task that occurs between rather than during such adventure scenes as combat, investigation, or theft. Each perk exempts you from one task; this can involve multiple skills, but you must have 16+ in any score for which you want the GM to waive success rolls. This most often affects “off-screen” rolls to get from A to B, as in this example:

Transporter: You needn’t make skill rolls to get your vehicle and its passengers and cargo safely through known friendly or neutral territory. Your vehicle operation skill, relevant Navigation specialty, and Area Knowledge for areas you’re crossing must all be at 16+.
