Some GURPS supplements – notably GURPS Low-Tech and High-Tech – describe armor that provides an n-in-6 chance of protecting a hit location. Add n if multiple items protect the hit location, and then roll 1d whenever the partially armored location is hit. On a roll less than or equal to n, DR protects.

Attackers can attempt to strike around partial armor. This gives a penalty in addition to that for hit location: -1 for 1/6 or 2/6, -2 for 3/6, -3 for 4/6, or -4 for 5/6. At “6/6” – full coverage – use Targeting Chinks in Armor (p. 400) instead.
