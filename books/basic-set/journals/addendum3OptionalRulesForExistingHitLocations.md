These rules alter those on p. 399 and p. 552. The GM may reserve extra dice rolls for randomly targeted attacks.

Skull: Targeted at -5 (not -7) from behind.

Face: Targeted at -7 (not -5) from behind. If hit from in front, roll 1d: A 1 means a skull hit if the attack was impaling, piercing, or tight-beam burning, a nose hit otherwise.

Neck: If hit with a cutting, impaling, piercing, or tight-beam burning attack, roll 1d: On a 1, it hits veins/arteries. On a crushing hit from behind, roll 1d: On a 1, it hits the spine. Crippling the spine this far up – a “broken neck” – causes Quadriplegic (p. 150), not Lame (Paraplegic), and is automatic if neck snap (p. 371) inflicts injury over HP! The GM may rule that Mortal Wounds doesn’t apply to cutting injury – any failed HT roll to avoid death means decapitation!

Torso (including Chest/Abdomen): If hit with a crushing, impaling, piercing, or tight-beam burning attack, roll 1d: On a 1, it hits the vitals. On a cutting hit from behind, roll 1d: On a 1, it hits the spine.

Vitals: Crushing attacks can target the “vitals” at -3. Wounding modifier is only ×1, but shock requires a HT roll for knockdown, at -5 if a major wound.

Arms and Legs: If hit, roll 1d: On a 1, a cutting, impaling, piercing, or tight-beam burning attack hits veins/arteries, while a crushing attack hits joints.

Hands and Feet: If hit with a crushing, cutting, piercing, or tight-beam burning attack, roll 1d: On a 1, the attack hits joints.
