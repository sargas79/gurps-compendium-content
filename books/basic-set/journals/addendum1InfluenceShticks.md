You can use a specific Influence skill through body language alone! You still have to make an Influence roll (p. 359) and your subject must be able to see you – you’re out of luck via phone, telepathy, etc. – but you can act without talking (so Voice and Disturbing Voice are irrelevant, and you can’t make demands more complex than “come here,” “go away,” “leave me be,” “let me go,” or “let me in”), physical contact, or appreciable time. Standard specialties are Convincing Nod for Fast-Talk, Disarming Smile for Diplomacy, Fearsome Stare for Intimidation, Gangster Swagger for Streetwise, Haughty Sneer for Savoir-Faire (High Society), and Sexy Pose for Sex Appeal.
