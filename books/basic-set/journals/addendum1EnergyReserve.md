**3 points/level**

This is a “power supply” for abilities of a specific origin (pp. 33-34) like chi, divine, magic, or psionic. Possibilities depend on the game world, and might be divided more finely (e.g., divine may be split into holy, unholy, druidic, and shamanic) – ask the GM. Write this as, for instance, “Energy Reserve 10 (Magical) [30].”

Energy Reserve (ER) works like Fatigue Points for the purposes of paying FP costs for advantages of the associated origin – be those costs built in, added by Costs Fatigue, or incurred by extra effort – and related skills, like spells for ER (Magical) or “cinematic martial-arts skills” for ER (Chi). It cannot fuel abilities of other origins or without specific origins, nor ordinary extra effort.

Beyond deliberate expenditure, only hostile powers of the ER’s origin – e.g., magical Fatigue Attacks or FP-theft spells for ER (Magical) – can deplete the ER. Poisons, missed sleep, exertion, etc., cannot sap it. Neither can powers not of the same origin; for instance, magical FP drains cannot deplete ER (Chi). Furthermore, low ER causes none of the bad effects of going below 1/3 FP, and having a full ER doesn’t protect against those effects.

You recharge 1 ER every 10 minutes, independent of rest. If you do rest, you recover FP at the same time. Most factors affecting FP recovery have no effect on ER recovery, but special skills or abilities of the ER’s origin affect it exactly as they do FP recovery. For instance, Recover Energy (p. 248) accelerates recovery of ER (Magical) and FP, and Damage Resistance modified with Absorption and Mana Sensitive, or Vampiric Bite with Mana Sensitive, can heal your choice of ER (Magical), FP, or HP.

### Special Limitations

Slow Recharge: Your ER recharges slowly. -20% for 1 ER/ hour; -60% for 1 ER/day.

Special Recharge: If your ER recharges on its own only under particular conditions, apply a suitable Accessibility limitation at half value (you always have access to the ER, but not to recharging). If your ER doesn’t recharge on its own and must be refilled using skills or abilities of its origin for energy theft rather than recovery (Vampiric Bite or an FP-stealing spell would work, but not Recover Energy), that’s -70%, and incompatible with Slow Recharge.
