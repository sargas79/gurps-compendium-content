A middle ground between Attack and All-Out Defense, representing a light jab or tap, or other “probing” attack. You must use a ready melee attack. You get -2 damage or -1 damage per die, whichever is worse. If you attempt to grab or grapple, your target defends at +1.

Movement: Step. Movement can come before or after the attack.

Active Defense: Any. Before attacking, select a defensive benefit. If you attack with a balanced weapon (including a hand or shield), you may choose either +1 to Parry with one weapon or +1 to Block; you can assign the bonus to the weapon you attack with. If you attack with an unbalanced weapon ( “U” on Parry), you may claim +1 to Parry with a different weapon or to Block, or opt to parry with the same weapon, at no bonus, despite using it to attack. If you kick, you get no active defense bonus but have +2 on DX rolls to avoid falling.
