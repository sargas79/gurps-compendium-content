Many new Talents have appeared – that explains GURPS Power-Ups 3: Talents – but here are 10 of the most popular:

Born Entertainer: Acting, Dancing, Performance, Public Speaking, Singing, and Stage Combat. Reaction bonus: crowds – they love you! 5 points/level.

Born to be Wired: Computer Hacking, Computer Operation, Computer Programming, Cryptography, Electronics Repair (Computers), and Expert Skill (Computer Security). Reaction bonus: hackers; people buying stock in your dot-com. 5 points/level.

Born War-Leader: Intelligence Analysis, Leadership, Savoir-Faire (Military), Strategy, and Tactics. Reaction bonus: military officers, tribal war-leaders, soldiers, and other professional warriors. 5 points/level.

Circuit Sense: Electrician, Electronics Operation, Electronics Repair, and Engineer (Electrical and Electronics). Reaction bonus: anyone for whom you use your skills. 5 points/level.

Craftiness: Acting, Camouflage, Disguise, Holdout, Shadowing, and Stealth. Reaction bonus: none – the entire point is that people don’t know you’re sneaky! 5 points/level.

Driver’s Reflexes: Boating, Driving, Piloting, and Submarine. Reaction bonus: passengers; gamblers betting on you in a race. 5 points/level.

Natural Athlete: Bicycling, Breath Control, Hiking, Jumping, Lifting, Running, Skating, Skiing, Sports, Swimming, and Throwing. Reaction bonus: sports fans, coaches, and recruiters. 10 points/level.

Natural Scientist: Astronomy, Biology, Chemistry, Expert Skill (Hydrology and Natural Philosophy), Geology, Mathematics (Applied, Statistics, and Surveying), Metallurgy, Meteorology, Paleontology, Physics, and Physiology. Reaction bonus: other scientists and anybody impressed by “smart people.” 10 points/level.

Social Scientist: Anthropology, Archaeology, Criminology, Economics, Expert Skill (Political Science), Geography, History, Philosophy, Psychology, Sociology, and Theology (Comparative). Reaction bonus: social and political thinkers, theorists, and activists. 10 points/level.

Street-Smart: Merchant, Panhandling, Scrounging, Shadowing, Streetwise, and Urban Survival. Reaction bonus: street denizens, especially hustlers. 5 points/level.
