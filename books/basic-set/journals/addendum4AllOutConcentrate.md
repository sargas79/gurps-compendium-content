Concentrate (p. 366), but more intense! You get +1 to all rolls for the ability, skill, or spell you’re concentrating on, at the cost of your active defenses. If your task requires multiple seconds of concentration, you may alternate between Concentrate and All-Out Concentrate, but you get the +1 only if you take All-Out Concentrate the entire time. If you’re knocked down, injured, or otherwise distracted, you must make a Will-2 roll (the usual Will-3, at +1 for this maneuver) to maintain concentration.

Movement: Step.

Active Defense: None, as for All-Out Attack (p. 365).
