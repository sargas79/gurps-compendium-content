**Variable**

Available only for switchable, beneficial abilities that you could normally leave “on” indefinitely (e.g., Insubstantiality). Your ability can operate for only a limited length of time. After that, it shuts down without warning, and you can’t reactivate it for five minutes. Limitation value depends on time limit:

| Time Limit | Modifier |
| --- | --- |
| Less than 30 seconds | -75% |
| Up to 1 minute | -65% |
| Up to 10 minutes | -50% |
| Up to 30 minutes | -25% |
| Up to 1 hour | -10% |
| Up to 12 hours (or one night) | -5% |
| Greater than 12 hours | -0% |
