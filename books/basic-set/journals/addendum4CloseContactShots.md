If you can touch your victim with a gun – Reach C for a Guns (Pistol) weapon, Reach C or 1 for any other firearm – All-Out Attack (Determined) gives +4 to hit, as with a melee attack, instead of +1. However, your target may attempt a parry (p. 376).

Pressing your gun against your target also gives you +4 to hit – cumulative with All-Out Attack, if you choose it – but your foe gets +2 to defend with a dodge or parry. Even if you’re behind them, they know your gun’s location and defend as though against a “runaround” attack (p. 391), at no bonus or penalty: This +2 cancels out the -2 for a “runaround.”

In all cases above, you never get a bonus from Acc, sights, aiming, etc. You can claim +1 from bracing if you hold the gun against your victim (as above) for at least one turn before shooting (treat as Evaluate), for a total of +5 (+9 with All-Out Attack).

Bulk normally applies in close combat because it’s assumed that you’re jostling for position with an active defender. If you’re standing behind an unresisting target (tied up, intimidated into surrender, etc.), you never suffer Bulk penalties to shoot them!
