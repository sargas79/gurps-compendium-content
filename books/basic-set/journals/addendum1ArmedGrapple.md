**Hard**

Defaults: Cloak, or other prerequisite skill-2.

Prerequisite: Cloak or appropriate Melee Weapon skill; cannot exceed prerequisite skill.

You can use a weapon to restrain an opponent in close combat, resulting in a grapple. To snag someone at full reach, use a weapon made for that, like a whip (p. 406).

A one-handed weapon other than a cloak can grapple only if you grip it in two hands first; this requires a Ready maneuver. To initiate an armed grapple with most weapons, roll against weapon skill at -2 – this attack is awkward and unconventional. However, it’s a standard move with a cloak (p. 404), and uses unpenalized Cloak skill. Use the hit location penalties for grappling (p. 370), not those for striking.

Your target may use any normal defense. If they fail to defend, you’ve successfully grappled them with your weapon. While using your weapon to grapple, you can neither attack nor defend with it. On your turn, you can follow up with a takedown, pin, choke, or Arm Lock (options depend on the body part grappled). Releasing the grapple is a free action.
