A Vision roll to see something in plain sight is at +10. But what does “in plain sight” mean? First, it means you’re actively looking in the right general direction: watching the road as you drive, keeping watch on your surroundings, stargazing, etc. Then it depends on the object of interest:

- An unconcealed light source in your arc of vision always counts. It’s hard not to spot the headlights of a car coming toward you (though not necessarily the car), an imprudent intruder’s flashlight (but not necessarily the intruder), the sun at day, the moon at night, etc. – although highly directional light sources (e.g., lasers or shuttered signal lamps) must be pointed at you to count.

- For objects other than light sources, it means that the Vision roll takes no penalties but those for distance and size – no modifiers for concealment, darkness, obscuration (like smoke), target posture, weather, etc. apply – and that no attempt (even a failed one!) was made to disguise or hide the object using concealment skills such as Camouflage, Holdout, or Stealth.

If you’ve already located a target with a successful Vision roll – with or without the +10 for plain sight – and want to discern small details (say, text or facial expressions), make another Vision roll at +20. This is instead of the +10. All normal Vision penalties apply.

The +10 and +20 above never apply:

- When not actively watching your environment. Happening to notice something in passing requires a Vision roll without these bonuses.

- In a Quick Contest against a concealment skill.

- To a vision-based skill roll – or when your Vision roll would be followed by an IQ or skill roll to gain information.

- To an attack roll, with or without Aim.
