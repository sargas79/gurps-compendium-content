Vision rolls in combat slow down the action, but the GM may require them whenever Visibility (p. 394) doesn’t apply but the enemy isn’t obvious:

- Against an SM -10 or smaller attacker.

Modifiers include the attacker’s SM and, as attacks draw attention, +10 for In Plain Sight (above); e.g., SM -14 requires a Vision-4 roll.

Failure means the defender doesn’t see the attack coming and treats the attacker as invisible: no active defense.

- Against a ranged attacker far enough away that distance gives -10 or worse (100+ yards). Modifiers include the range penalty and +10 for plain sight; e.g., 200 yards (-12 for range) requires a Vision-2 roll. Again, failure permits no defense. This matters when distant snipers shoot into the action!

The GM may also apply these rules whenever distance and size penalties total -10 or worse. Never roll when Vision is at a bonus, though – even +1.

Surprise ranged attacks from concealment simply grant no active defense. After the first attack, defenders may try Vision rolls with modifiers for distance, visibility, etc., but no +10. Success allows normal defenses; failure permits only a dodge at -4 (exactly as for Visibility).
