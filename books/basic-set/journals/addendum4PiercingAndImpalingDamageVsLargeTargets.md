Bolts, cannonballs, bullets, and other impaling and piercing attacks – particularly if massed or at high rates of fire – tend to chew up vehicles (especially ships and aircraft) unrealistically. Tweaking Injury to Unliving, Homogenous, and Diffuse Targets (p. 380) for high-SM targets can solve this problem!

Unliving targets use these wounding modifiers:

| SM | pi - | pi | pi+ | pi++ or imp |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Up to +4 | ×1/5\* | ×1/3\* | ×1/2\* | ×1\* |
| +5-6 | ×1/10 | ×1/5 | ×1/3 | ×1/2 |
| +7-8 | ×1/20 | ×1/10 | ×1/5 | ×1/3 |
| +9-10 | ×1/50 | ×1/20 | ×1/10 | ×1/5 |
| +11-12 | ×1/100 | ×1/50 | ×1/20 | ×1/10 |
| +13 or more | ×1/200 | ×1/100 | ×1/50 | ×1/20 |

\* Standard wounding modifier.

Homogenous targets shift down one row (to a maximum of “SM +13 or more”); e.g., treat a SM +5-6 Homogenous target the same way as a SM +7-8 Unliving one.
