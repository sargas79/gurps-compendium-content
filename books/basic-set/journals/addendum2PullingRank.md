A campaign that uses these rules often will benefit from the fuller treatment in GURPS Social Engineering: Pulling Rank.

Some adventurers serve organizations that support their adventures more than typical employers would. These may take the form of Patrons (p. 72) or Contact Groups (p. 44): The player requests help and the GM rolls against frequency of appearance, with success indicating assistance.

For hierarchical organizations, however, Rank (p. 29) fits better. The GM can use its level as a guide to who and what the PC can command to meet organizational goals. But unlike Patron, Rank provides neither a clear sense of organizational power nor anything like the appearance-roll mechanic for resolving requests.

A workaround is to merge the concepts of Rank and Associated NPCs (p. 31). In this approach, Rank determines where the PC stands and what they can ask for, serving as an effective Patron.
