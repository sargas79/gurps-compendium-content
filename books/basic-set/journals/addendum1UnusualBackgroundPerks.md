Some Unusual Backgrounds (p. 96) are so minor as to be perks. Examples are Extra Option (entitlement to use a beneficial optional rule not otherwise used in the campaign), Rules Exemption (immunity to a detrimental optional rule used in the campaign), Secret Knowledge (access to a specific skill with controlled access), Special Exercises (right to exceed a human norm, usually a maximum attribute or advantage level, by one level per perk), and Unusual Training (access to one skill or technique that otherwise requires a special advantage like Trained by a Master). The GM may restrict when, where, and how especially powerful or unrealistic rules or abilities work.
