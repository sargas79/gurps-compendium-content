**Hard**

Default: ST-4; cannot exceed ST+3.

This brute-force attack consists of grabbing and suddenly twisting the victim’s limb – see Neck Snap or Wrench Limb (p. 404). The technique defaults to ST, not a skill, and must be learned separately for each limb: Wrench Arm, Wrench Leg, and so forth. Wrestling gives its usual skill-based ST bonus.
