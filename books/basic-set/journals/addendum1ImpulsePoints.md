**5 points/level**

Many optional rules let players spend bonus character points in play (not to improve their characters!), including Buying Success and Player Guidance (p. 347), Flesh Wounds (p. 417), Character-Point Powered Abilities (p. 325), and everything in GURPS Power-Ups 5: Impulse Buys. At the GM’s option, players can pay these costs using Impulse Points (IP) as well as or instead of character points. Nobody can spend more IP than they currently have – IP cannot “go negative” – but they can make up any cost shortfall with character points.

Unlike character points, IP refresh. At the start of each game session, anyone with less than their full number of IP regains 1 IP. Those at their maximum stay there – it’s impossible to begin a game session with IP above one’s maximum.

The GM decides how many IP are allowed. If the GM wants a dramatic campaign, they might make a certain number of IP a mandatory campaign advantage or even a 0-point feature (like ST 10 and SM 0 for humans).
