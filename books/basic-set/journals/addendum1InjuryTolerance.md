A new form suits many supers and monsters:

Damage Reduction: After subtracting DR from damage and applying any wounding modifiers, the injury you suffer is subject to a divisor. Round up – minimum injury from an attack that pierces DR is 1 HP. This costs 50 points for a divisor of 2, 75 points for 3, 100 points for 4, and 125 points for 5. To price a divisor of 10 or more, find it in the “Linear Measurement” column of the Size and Speed/Range Table (p. 550), add 2 to the corresponding entry in the “Size” column, and multiply the sum by 25. This gives 150 points for a divisor of 10, 300 points for 100, 450 points for 1,000, and so on. Common modifiers are Limited (see Limited Defenses, p. 46) and Cosmic, Rounds down, +50% (which removes the minimum 1 HP injury).
