**+10%**

This exists only for physiological features (e.g., Claws), defenses, and other traits that are normally “always on.” It lets you switch your advantage off and on with a Ready maneuver. Specify whether it switches on or off – or remains in its current state – when you’re knocked out.

You can’t add Switchable to an advantage that lists special modifiers that enable you to turn it off and on, regardless of whether your ability has those modifiers. If a trait notes Switchable as a special enhancement with a different value, use that value instead. You can’t add it to meta-traits, either – for a switchable meta-trait, buy Alternate Form.
