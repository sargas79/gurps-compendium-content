When you use an All-Out Attack (Determined, Feint, or Strong) for a slam – including a flying tackle, pounce, or shield rush – you may travel up to full Move, although you can still only move forward.

If you choose Double, you can’t slam twice but can make another melee attack before you slam, either on the slam’s target or on somebody passed along the way. This allows only half Move, as usual for All-Out Attack.
