**Hard**

Defaults: Brawling-1 or Karate-1.\* Prerequisite: Brawling or Karate; cannot exceed prerequisite skill.\* This is a close-combat strike using the forehead. Natural targets are the face (-5) and skull (-7) of a standing adversary, but you can attack anything! However, while the skull is hard, it has poor reach and leverage.

Roll against Head Butt to hit. Damage is thrust-1 crushing. Your victim may defend normally; if they parry, any damage this causes affects your face. Self-inflicted injury for a target with DR 3+ applies to the skull; however, your skull’s DR 2 protects normally. If you have rigid head protection – e.g., a metal helm – add +1 to damage and apply your armor’s DR against self-inflicted injury.

\* Those without either skill strike at DX-2 and do thrust-2 damage.
