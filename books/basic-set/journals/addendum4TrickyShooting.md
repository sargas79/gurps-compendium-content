The GM may extend certain options for melee combat to ranged combat:

Prediction Shot: To simulate “leading the target” or reading an adversary to deduce where they’ll jump or weave, permit Deceptive Attack (p. 369). The resulting defense penalty reduces Dodge only.

Ranged Feint: For mind games with a target – e.g., faking a shot to draw their guard, or suddenly changing who or what a weapon is pointed at – allow Feint or All-Out Attack (Feint) (p. 365). This is possible only with a weapon the attacker could use to attack their victim, who must be aware of the attacker (unaware targets simply get no defense!). All modifiers that apply to ranged attacks also apply to ranged feints.

Ranged Rapid Strike: To let shooters engage two different enemies with a RoF 2+ weapon – subject to the target restrictions in the first paragraph of Spraying Fire (p. 409) – permit Rapid Strike (p. 370). This is at the usual -6 to skill. The shooter chooses how to split up RoF, and picks hit locations and other combat options separately for each target. Cannot be combined with Dual-Weapon Attack (p. 417).
