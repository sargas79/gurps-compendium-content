You can inflict one specific disadvantage on yourself by rolling against HT (if the trait is physical (P)) or Will (if mental (M)), at -1 per additional attempt per hour. It’s up to you to make this useful. The GM decides what disadvantages to allow, but you cannot have both the controllable and regular form of a disadvantage, nor select a disadvantage that already includes similar rules, like Berserk or Epilepsy.
