GURPS High-Tech and Ultra-Tech standardize TL6-8 batteries and TL9-12 power cells for gadgets. Most other GURPS supplements state uptime or shots in terms of these.

The GM may allow substitutions: Divide (multiply) the number of batteries or cells required – or multiply (divide) uptime or shots – by 10 per line down (up) on the table, or by 2 per TL by which the battery or cell’s TL exceeds (falls short of) the device’s TL.

#### Batteries (TL6-8)

Non-rechargeable batteries. Rechargeable batteries cost 5× as much.

| Type | Cost | Weight | Sample Uses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tiny (T) | $0.25 | 0.02 lb. | watches, bugs |
| Extra-Small (XS) | $0.50 | 0.1 lb. | cameras, night-vision goggles |
| Small (S) | $1 | 0.33 lb. | flashlights, portable radios |
| Medium (M) | $5 | 2 lbs. | lanterns, laptop computers |
| Large (L) | $10 | 10 lbs. | motorcycles, base-camp radios |
| Very Large (VL) | $20 | 50 lbs. | golf carts, cars |

#### Power Cells (TL9+)

Rechargeable or non-rechargeable cells. Non-rechargeables get double uptime or shots, after which they are useless.
