You own a weapon that’s uniquely suited to you – specify it when you take the perk. When you use this weapon, you’re at +1 to effective skill and all techniques based on it. This is largely a matter of intimate familiarity.

You must acquire your weapon normally; e.g., with money or as Signature Gear. It can be of any quality, and this perk changes neither the weapon nor its price. If you lose the weapon, you lose this perk – although you can buy a new Weapon Bond in play.
