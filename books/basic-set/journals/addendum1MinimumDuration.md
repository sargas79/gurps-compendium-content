**Variable**

Available only for switchable abilities that would – in the GM’s opinion – seriously inconvenience you if you couldn’t deactivate them at will. Your ability must stay “on” for a certain period of time once activated; you can’t shut it off sooner. Minimum Duration can never exceed Maximum Duration (if any). Limitation value depends on the minimum time your ability stays on:

| Minimum Time | Modifier |
| --- | --- |
| Less than 1 hour | -0% |
| Up to 8 hours | -5% |
| Up to 12 hours (or one night) | -10% |
| Up to 24 hours | -15% |
| Up to 1 week | -20% |
| Up to 1 month | -25% |
| Greater than 1 month | -30% |

On an advantage that allows Always On (p. 110), this limitation is worth at most -5% less than Always On; e.g., if Always On is -20%, Minimum Duration stops at -15%.
