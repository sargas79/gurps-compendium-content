The GM may permit “dramatic” or “emergency” abilities that burn unspent character points. These are much cheaper: Build the ability normally, divide its point cost by 5, and round up. Such an ability is inert except when invoked by spending points in accordance with how well it suits the story:

- Use fits the scene perfectly – it’s dramatically appropriate and in line with the GM’s desires. 1 point.

- Use is believable given the PC’s other abilities, and can be worked into the story with effort. 2 points.

- Use is about the player showing off or making gains despite the storyline. From 3 points to the character point-powered ability’s cost (i.e., 1/5 of full cost), depending on the level of disruption.

The GM decides what qualifies as a “use”: Usually one scene (battle, verbal exchange, long and uneventful trip, etc.) – but maybe just a turn or single stunt for an instantaneous show of force.
