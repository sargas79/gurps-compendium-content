**Variable**

These gifts replace long lists of languages (p. 23), whether as a cinematic convenience or as supernatural (Su) or exotic (X) abilities. The GM decides which advantages exist. In all cases, the GM may rule that you need a suitable Hidden Lore roll to use – or cannot use – a language to which you couldn’t possibly have been exposed: forgotten, invented, secret, etc.

Universal Translator: You can understand any language you encounter, with Native-level fluency and literacy. This works only with one language beyond your native one at a time. You need one second of concentration to switch languages. 30 points.

Omnilingual: You don’t speak all of the Earth’s 5,000 or so languages, but each new language you encounter turns out to be one you’ve studied. You enjoy Accented-level fluency and literacy – or Native-level, if you also have Language Talent (p. 65). You can use several languages at once without concentrating to switch between them. 40 points.

Xeno-Omnilingual: As Omnilingual, but covering all of the campaign’s planets, dimensions, etc. Languages spoken by beings with exotic vocal organs may require Mimicry (p. 68) and an IQ roll for you to speak them. 80 points.
