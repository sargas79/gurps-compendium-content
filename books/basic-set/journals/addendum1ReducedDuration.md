**Variable**

Your ability lasts for a shorter time than it should. Limitation value depends on the duration reduction:

| Multiple | Modifier | Multiple | Modifier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1/2 duration | -5% | 1/20 duration | -25% |
| 1/3 duration | -10% | 1/30 duration | -30% |
| 1/6 duration | -15% | 1/60 duration | -35% |
| 1/10 duration | -20% |  |  |

You can extend the table from the progression shown, but you cannot take an ability’s minimum duration below one second. For example, Affliction (Disadvantage) has a variable duration that lasts a minimum of one minute, and thus is eligible for at worst Reduced Duration, 1/60 – but unmodified Affliction, with a minimum duration of one second, cannot have any level of Reduced Duration.

Advantages with a “maintained” duration, such as Mind Control, cannot have this limitation.
