If an ability offers specific rules for extra effort (e.g., spending FP to improve the range of Clairsentience or the activation roll for Warp), those supersede these rules!

The GM may let heroes push abilities that require active use past their ordinary limits. Extra effort attempts to increase a quantifiable effect, like advantage level, damage dice, or top speed – or even the DR of a force field, if the GM feels that’s sufficiently “active.” Make a Will roll at -1 per 5% increase in effect, or fraction thereof (maximum 100%). The usual +5 for motivation by strong emotions counts, but there’s no penalty for missing FP. If using a power, add a bonus equal to its Talent (see Other Powers, p. 257).

Barring critical success, extra effort costs FP. Instantaneous feats (e.g., attacks) cost a flat 1 FP per use. For maintainable abilities, roll every minute, at 1 FP per roll. These FP are spent after the Will roll and before any FP cost or roll the ability requires.

Success grants the desired increase in effect (but doesn’t guarantee success at using the ability). Critical success waives the FP cost for extra effort. Failure means the ability works exactly as it would have without extra effort. On a critical failure, the task being attempted fails automatically (and on an 18, it critically fails automatically).

For extra effort in combat, roll your attack or defense – not against Will.
