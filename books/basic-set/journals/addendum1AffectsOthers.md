**+50% per person**

You can extend your advantage’s benefits to others. If you can affect a limited number of willing subjects by touching them, Affects Others costs +50% per person. There’s no effect on unwilling recipients! You must take a separate Ready maneuver to make contact with each companion, who must be within reach. This grants the advantage’s effects, not the ability to use it. You turn it “off” and “on,” set its level, and so on, and your decisions apply equally to you and all subjects.

Your ability affects others only while they’re touching you. They can end its effects by breaking contact with you. To affect those who are merely nearby, add Area Effect – in which case a Ready maneuver lets you affect any number of people up to your limit within your area.

As described above, Affects Others is for movement abilities like Flight and physical transformations like Shrinking. It’s a good way to keep the PCs together on adventures that would leave behind those who can’t fly, shrink, etc. The GM may allow Affects Others on other traits when it serves this purpose.

If you apply Force Field (p. 47) to Damage Resistance or another defense, you may add Affects Others for a flat +50% if you also take any level of Area Effect. Together, these three modifiers let you extend your advantage’s protection to everyone inside your area. This force field works against attacks and hazards crossing it from the outside. Threats inside the field bypass its protection.

A force field based on Damage Resistance impedes movement. Anyone who wishes to cross it must force their way in. Those who try this as a free action (allowed once per turn) roll thrust damage. Those who use Attack, Move and Attack, or All-Out Attack to blast their way in roll their attack’s usual damage. Damage in excess of DR makes an opening large enough to step through. This closes at the end of their turn, and doesn’t weaken the force field.

The GM shouldn’t allow Affects Others on advantages that let the user do something, such as Healing, Innate Attack, or Mind Control. To bestow forbidden traits or affect unwilling subjects, buy Affliction with the Advantage enhancement.
