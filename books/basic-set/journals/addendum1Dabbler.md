You know a little about several related skills – but not enough to have a full point in any of them. Select eight skills (which must have defaults) that you can use at +1 to the usual attribute default. You can trade two choices for one at default+2, or four for one at default+3. If a skill has an unusually generous default, you can’t raise it to the level that points in the skill would buy.

Example: A schoolteacher dabbles in Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics (Applied), and Physics. All four default to IQ-6. They choose Biology and Chemistry at IQ-5, trade two choices for Physics at IQ-4, and exchange four choices for Mathematics at IQ-3.

These skills still count as defaults, not as studied.

You can’t default other skills to the improved defaults, nor benefit from rules that require you to “know” a skill.
