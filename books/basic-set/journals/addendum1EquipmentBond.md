You own a piece of equipment (e.g., sensor or vehicle) or a kit (tool, medical, etc.) that’s uniquely suited to you – specify it when you take the perk. When you use that gear, you get +1 to the skill associated with it, regardless of its quality, cumulative with any bonus inherent to the hardware. This represents being used to your stuff or having assembled your kit.

You must acquire this equipment normally; e.g., with money or as Signature Gear. If you lose it, you lose the perk – though you can acquire a new Equipment Bond in play.
