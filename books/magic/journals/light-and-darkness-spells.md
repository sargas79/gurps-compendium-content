These spells affect not just visible light, but also infrared and ultraviolet light. Spells that provide illumination will allow those with Infravision and Ultravision to see, while spells that block ordinary vision will also block those senses.

Many Light spells can produce varying levels of light. If a Light spell is used as the sole source of illumination, the following Vision modifiers are appropriate.

| Intensity | Vision Modifier |
| --- | --- |
| Starlight | -7 |
| Candlelight or moonlight | -5 |
| Torchlight | -3 |
| Daylight | 0 |
