You own a piece of equipment – or a kit (tool, survival, medical, etc.) – that’s uniquely suited to you. When you use it, you have +1 to effective skill regardless of the gear’s actual quality. This is cumulative with bonus for high-quality equipment.

Like Weapon Bond (p. 250), this isn’t a magical enchantment or spiritual attunement. The equipment simply has the perfect level of comfort, fit, balance, and so on. If the gear is lost or destroyed, the Equipment Bond doesn’t transfer to new hardware – but you can acquire a new Equipment Bond in play.

Each Equipment Bond is a 1-point perk. You must pay the usual price for the equipment, but you can acquire it as Signature Gear (p. B85).
