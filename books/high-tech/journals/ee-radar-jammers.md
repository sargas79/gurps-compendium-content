**Radar Jammer (TL7).** A jammer tuned to radar frequencies, operating to “blind” the radar system; often carried in EW aircraft to provide cover for other aircraft. Effectively similar to a radio jammer, but works against Electronics Operation (Sensors) rather than Electronics Operation (Comm). Radius 15 miles. Major appliance power. $50,000, 200 lbs. 1941. At TL8, double radius and halve weight.

**Radar Spoofer (TL8).** An advanced variation on radar jamming, based on digital radio-frequency memory. Rather than simply blinding a radar system, it records its output and sends back a modified version that gives a misleading impression of a target’s location and size. Requires a Quick Contest of Electronics Operation (EW) vs. Electronics Operation (Sensors). Treat as a TL8 radar jammer with ×2 cost. [1975] 1999.
