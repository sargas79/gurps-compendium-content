At early TL5, nearly all pistols are single-shot weapons. By mid-TL5, multi-barrel designs are available. Non-repeating pistols become rare after the introduction of the metallic cartridge – people generally prefer to have more than one or two shots if possible! Non-repeating designs produced at TL6-8 are mostly special-purpose firearms for which the number of shots isn’t a top priority. Such weapons include holdout guns, which above all must be small, and target pistols, which most of all must be accurate.
