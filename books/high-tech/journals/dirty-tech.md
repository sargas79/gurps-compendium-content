High technology isn’t always manufactured from carbon fiber, packed with microchips, or glistening with chrome and style. Sometimes, a hero is desperate enough to try almost anything. What would MacGyver or the Professor do in a pinch? Can you fashion a battery on a deserted tropical island, or cobble together functional armor from the contents of the high school cafeteria? Maybe you can! This is “dirty tech.”

Throughout this book are Dirty Tech boxes that contain hasty solutions for hard-up heroes: homemade explosives, field-expedient firearms, jury-rigged electronics, etc. Been aching to use that skill languishing on your character sheet? Wondering why you bothered with that point in Armoury, Chemistry, Electronics Repair, or Machinist? Check out the Dirty Tech entries!
