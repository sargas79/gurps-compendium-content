Intruders will try to crack even the best security. With the right gear, they might just succeed!

“Burglar’s tools” consist principally of hardware with legitimate applications. Hand Tools (pp. 24-26), Power Tools (pp. 26-27), Rescue Tools (pp. 29-30), and Conventional Explosives (pp. 183-187) describe many handy items. An endoscope (p. 209) gives +2 on rolls to crack locks, safes, and anything else where seeing the normally hidden insides would be useful. A stethoscope (pp. 213, 222) is basic equipment for safecracking and – in a very quiet environment – good for +2 on rolls to defeat security devices where hearing the mechanism would help (including mechanical locks).
