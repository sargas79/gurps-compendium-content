A well-equipped home or professional workshop has a variety of electrically powered devices. The smaller ones are portable.

**Shopvac (TL6).** A larger vacuum cleaner able to clean up wet areas (including crime scenes, if it’s carried away after use; -2 to subsequent Forensics rolls). Household power. $130, 30 lbs. 1905.

**Power Drill (TL6).** A handheld tool in which a small electric motor turns a drill tip. A typical model inflicts 1d+2(2) pi++ damage on materials such as wood and plaster. The standard design has a trigger switch and pistol grip; early models have clumsier arrangements (-2 to skill). Household power. $20, 4.5 lbs. [1895] 1917.
