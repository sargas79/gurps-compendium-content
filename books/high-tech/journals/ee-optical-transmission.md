Another way to send signals is on beams of light, directed at photoelectric sensors to produce varying current flow. Such beams can be more directional than radio. Early versions used reflected sunlight; this was followed by communicators based on arc lights (p. 20), and much later, on lasers (p. 5). After the development of optical fiber in 1970, fiber-optic communication came into commercial use in the 1980s, increasingly replacing wire-based signals throughout TL8.
