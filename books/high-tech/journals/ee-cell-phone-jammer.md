**Cell-Phone Jammer (TL8).** A special-purpose system that interferes with all cell-phone calls within a 15-yard radius. A system with double the radius has 4× cost and weight. This functions as a broad-spectrum jammer for cell-phone frequencies. Rather than requiring an Electronics Operation (Comm) roll to use the phone, overcoming the jamming requires a Hearing roll at -2 to make out what is being said. Automotive or household power. $500, 5 lbs. 1991.
