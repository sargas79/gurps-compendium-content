Radio waves at frequencies from 1.5 to 30 megahertz can “skip” off the upper atmosphere. One skip can reach up to 2,000 miles; each additional skip gives -1 to Electronics Operation (Comm) – six skips (-5) can pick up a transmission from anywhere on Earth. A large antenna (p. 28) is necessary for transmission. Apply -2 for each of unfavorable time of day (day for 1.5-12 megahertz, night for 12-30 megahertz), summer, and solar flares. Cost and weight are unchanged, but transmitter and receiver must both be shortwave. [1921] 1924.
