As explained under Fencing Parries (p. 122), the inability of the rapier, saber, and smallsword to parry flails (see p. B405) is a consequence of blade design, not weapon skill. Any weapon that works with the Main-Gauche, Rapier, Saber, or Smallsword skill and at least one Melee Weapon skill other than those four can parry flails at the usual penalties. This includes the edged rapier (and any other rapier that weighs 3 lbs. or more), jian, jutte, sai, and short staff (which is identical to a baton).
