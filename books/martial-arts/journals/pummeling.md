You can strike with the pommel or hilt of any fencing weapon or sword, or with the butt of a reach C or 1 melee weapon. Roll against DX-1, Brawling-1, or Karate-1 to hit. You may substitute Hammer Fist with a one-handed weapon or Two-Handed Punch with a two-handed one, if better. Use full DX, Boxing, Brawling, or Karate skill to punch with a tonfa or with a sword that sports a knuckle guard (backsword, cutlass, etc.). Reach is always C. In all cases, damage is thrust crushing (like brass knuckles) plus skill bonuses, +1 if using two hands.
