There’s no need to limit superhuman abilities to chi powers and cinematic skills and techniques in a Martial Arts game. Gamers expect magic in fantasy and psionics in space opera. Regardless of genre, such elements are an excellent match for the myths associated with some styles. The GM should also consider the role of nonhumans and supernatural events. These might be the campaign’s focus or inexplicable elements on its fringes, but both feature prominently in martial-arts legend. For details about the folklore and remarkable claims surrounding particular arts, see Chapter 5.
