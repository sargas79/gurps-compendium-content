Breaking Blow normally only affects unarmed attacks. However, legendary warriors routinely use massive axes, mauls, and swords to lay waste to castles and splinter mighty trees. In a high-powered cinematic game, the GM may permit those who know Breaking Blow and Forced Entry (p. B196) to use this skill when attacking inanimate objects with melee weapons (and even battering rams!).
