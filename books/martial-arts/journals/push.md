You may opt to substitute Push for ST (not skill) whenever you attempt an unarmed technique intended to knock an opponent down or back without grappling or injuring him. For shove-like techniques, use Push’s special knockback-only “damage” instead of the usual roll. These benefits affect Push Kick (p. 78), Sweep (p. 81), Trip (p. 81), and any shove- or sweep-based technique (see Basic Attacks, p. 59).
