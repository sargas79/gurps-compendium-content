Use Knot-Tying to tie up a foe after entangling him with a whip (p. B406) or a lariat (p. B411). You must enter close combat with him and roll a Regular Contest of Knot-Tying vs. his DX or best grappling skill each turn. This counts as an Attack. If you win, you bind his arms to his sides. If you lose, he’s free of your weapon.

See Binding (pp. 82-83) for a more cinematic way to tie someone up with this skill.
