Judo is the generic skill of “advanced” unarmed grappling. It’s part of any style that incorporates a systematic body of grabs, grapples, sweeps, and throws, regardless of the style’s provenance. It isn’t uniquely associated with the Judo style (p. 166); in fact, that art teaches the Judo Sport skill, not Judo!

Sumo Wrestling and Wrestling don’t lend their ST bonuses to Judo techniques – even techniques that also default to those skills. Unless a rule explicitly states otherwise, you only receive these bonuses when using Sumo Wrestling or Wrestling, or their techniques.
