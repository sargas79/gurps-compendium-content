These consist of numerous “nodes” –computers and communications systems connected on a permanent or semi-permanent basis. They can range from local intranets linking a handful of individuals to galactic information webs.

Ultra-tech networks generally combine message relay and data access functions, allowing people to store and find information on the network as well as using it to transmit and receive data.

Data networks usually store and retransmit a variety of information (news, knowledge, personal mail, discussion groups, etc.), using either decentralized or centralized computers and data storage systems.

Some groups may set up their own private networks, but larger networks run by telecom companies or the government usually exist. Subscription costs are infinitely variable; those given below are only suggestions.
