### Tunable Mind Disruptors

Add +50% to cost for each extra setting that a mind disruptor possesses. It takes one second and a Ready maneuver to change settings.

### Psionic Neutralizer

If psychotronic technology exists, psionic neutralizer is another possible setting for neural disruptors or mind disruptors. This beam scrambles the areas of the target’s nervous system that control psi powers. Anyone struck loses all his psionic powers for a number of minutes equal to the margin of failure.
