Personal Sidearm: 4mm Gauss pistol or laser pistol; sonic stunners.

Paramilitary or Special Ops: 4mm Gauss PDW, heavy laser pistol, gyroc carbine, or laser carbine; 40mm electromagnetic grenade launcher; snipers use a 10mm portable railgun, a dinosaur laser, or a laser rifle.

Infantry: 4mm Gauss rifle or laser rifle, with 25mm underbarrel EMGL; HEMP grenades are popular.

Squad Support or Powered Infantry: 4mm Gauss LSW, 15mm portable railgun, 40mm EMGL, Gatling laser, or MLAWS missile launcher.

Holdout: Gauss needler, holdout laser.

Melee Weapons: Neurolash; vibroblade knife; in superscience settings, the monowire switchblade is highly effective.
