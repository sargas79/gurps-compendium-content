This book assumes that artificial intelligence is a software-based phenomena; the only hardware requirement is the necessary Complexity to run the computer software described in Artificial Intelligence (p. 25). However, this isn’t the only option.

Neural Net: A Volitional AI (p. 25) program may be incapable of running on normal machines. It may require a machine specifically dedicated to cognition, e.g., a neural net similar to the human brain. If so, double the cost of the AI’s computer hardware.

Quantum Thinkers: In some universes, the human mind – and by extension, machines that can duplicate it – requires more than just good software. If thought involves quantum mechanical effects, then volitional AI will only run on a quantum computer (which will significantly increase its expense).
