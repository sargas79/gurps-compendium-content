Cybernetics may be implanted in someone against their will and remotely controlled. This won’t cost the recipient any character points, since it isn’t an advantage for him. Usually only brain implants are designed for remote control, but other options are possible, such as remote-controlled bionic arms, ripsnakes, cyberhair, or digital skin.

Communicators can always be remotely controlled. Other cybernetics will need a remote-controlled communicator implant (any type) or an implanted digital mind (p. 216).
