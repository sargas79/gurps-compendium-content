GURPS Ultra-Tech is intended to spark adventures or open up story possibilities. The tech level, superscience indicator, technology progression, and technology path rules are there to provide guidance, but it is up to the GM to decide what is and is not available in a campaign. All technologies in GURPS Ultra-Tech are completely optional. The GM should use whichever items seem fun, ignore those that don’t, and change statistics as needed to fit his own campaign or play style.
