Robot characters are created by choosing (or designing) a robot body template. The robot templates in GURPS Ultra-Tech represent general classes of machines rather than particular models.

Each template comes with a set of lenses that represent particular designs. Each robot template must include a machine intelligence lens (below). Other lenses are optional. Many robots are built to resemble a living creature, and have a biomorphic lens (p. 28). Most robot templates include a series of TL lenses representing design improvements from advancing technology.
