Some gadgets have a notation “adjust for SM” after their weight, cost, and power requirement. This means the weight, cost, and number of power cells are multiplied by a factor depending on the user’s Size Modifier. For ordinary-sized humans (SM 0) there is no change. However, if used by larger or smaller individuals, or if added to vehicles or robots with a higher or lower SM, multiply as follows:

Devices that must be aimed are difficult to operate remotely. A gun with a communicator plugged into it could fire, but unless it also had a plugged-in sensor, the firer wouldn’t know whether there was a target. And unless a gun with a sensor was attached to something like a powered tripod (p. 151), it could only be fired at someone who crossed its sights. As always, the GM should rule on whether a particular piece of gadget programming is possible.
