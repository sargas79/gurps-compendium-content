Every TL9+ firearm has a laser sight (p. 149), a HUD link (below), a recognition grip or transponder ring (p. 150), and a diagnostic computer (p. 151) for free. These electronics are powered by an integral B cell, which is included in the basic weight of the gun. A tiny computer (p. 22) may also be included. However, these components can be ignored if they are not standard in a particular setting.
