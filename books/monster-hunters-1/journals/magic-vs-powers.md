Magic (pp. 32-39) can't interfere directly with powers. Bioenhancement (pp. 40-42) is as integral to the user's body as a heartbeat, Mysticism (pp. 42-45) is channeled from a much higher source than mere magic, and psionics (pp. 45-48) use a completely different type of energy. Magic can interfere with powers' effects, however; e.g., if Temperature Control (p. 47) is heating an area up, Lesser Control Energy can cool it back down.
