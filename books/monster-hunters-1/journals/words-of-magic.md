Magic is both an art and a science. With the right combination of words, motions, and mindset, a person can force the universe to bend to his will. Ritual Path magic is a new system for GURPS - one which allows a caster to attempt almost any effect . . . at least, in theory.

The following rules assume that the caster is an adept (Words of Magic, below). If not, see Non-Adept Magic (p. 36).

**adept:** Someone with the Ritual Adept (p. 24) advantage (e.g., a witch). All adepts are also mages.

**caster:** Someone using a Path skill to perform a ritual. Short for "spellcaster." Anyone with at least one point in Thaumatology skill may be a caster; mages and adepts are just better at it.

**mage:** Someone with the Magery advantage (e.g., a sage or witch).

**Path skill:** One of the nine skills listed under Learning Magic (below). Limited by both the caster's Thaumatology skill and his Magery level.

**ritual:** A specific magical effect (e.g., "Put that security guard to sleep").

**spell:** See ritual.
