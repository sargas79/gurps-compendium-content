Champions have to be extremely competent. This is why they're built on a high point total - so they can afford their abilities without having to take a ton of disadvantages. Because of this, every template in Chapter 1 is built with -70 points in disadvantages: -55 points from the template and -15 points from the lens (Why We Fight, p. 7). This becomes a total of -75 points when the player chooses -5 points in quirks. Heroes built "from scratch," using the rules in this chapter, should also have a -75-point limit, following the same -55/-15/-5 breakdown regardless of power level.
